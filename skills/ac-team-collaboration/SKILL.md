# Team collaboration

## Overview

A team is a group of people who must coordinate to produce something none could produce alone. Most team dysfunction is not a talent problem but a coordination problem: unclear norms, meetings that waste time, unspoken disagreement, feedback withheld until it explodes. Collaboration craft is the set of working agreements, communication habits, and repair practices that let a team coordinate and disagree without fragmenting. The goal is not harmony — a team that never disagrees is usually hiding something — but the ability to surface and resolve disagreement in the work's service.

This skill owns the day-to-day collaboration of a human team. Its neighbours carve adjacent coordination: ac-handoff writes the artifact that hands work between sessions; ac-inbound-triage routes an incoming work queue; ac-multi-agent-orchestration coordinates agents on one job. None of them owns the human team's working agreements, safety, feedback, and conflict repair.

Primary sources: <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_safety> and <https://doi.org/10.2307/2666999>.

## When to use

- When a team needs explicit working agreements or better meetings.
- When a distributed team must communicate across time zones.
- When people stay silent in meetings or bad news travels slowly.
- When feedback is avoided, or conflict is simmering unresolved.

## When NOT to use

- For writing or resuming a handoff artifact (use ac-handoff).
- For routing and triaging inbound work (use ac-inbound-triage).
- For splitting one job across multiple agents (use ac-multi-agent-orchestration).

## Status and honest limits

Collaboration craft creates conditions, not guarantees: a team with good norms can still fail on talent, resourcing, or strategy. Psychological safety is not the absence of standards or conflict — the safest teams often disagree the most openly. These practices are context-dependent: what works for a small co-located team needs adapting for a large distributed one, and no single framework fits every culture. Nothing here substitutes for addressing a genuine performance or conduct problem through the appropriate channel; collaboration craft is not a way to avoid a hard decision about a person.
